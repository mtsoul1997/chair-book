# Chair Book

A personal scheduling book for barbers and stylists who book their own clients by call and text.
No booking service, no prices, no payments. Just your week, your regulars, your client cards and a history that never gets erased.

## Get it on your phone
Every push to `main` builds the app automatically (Actions tab).
When it finishes, open **Releases** on your Android phone and tap `ChairBook.apk` to install.

## What's inside
- `www/index.html` — the whole app (also runs as a web page)
- `android/` — the Android project (Capacitor)
- `assets/` — icon and splash source images

## Features
Weekly schedule, regulars that repeat every week, client cards with notes and birthdays,
permanent cut history with calendar and search, "due for a cut" list with a ready-to-send text,
reminder texts from your own number, book-again in one tap, blocked time and days off,
reminder notifications and a morning lineup, and backup/restore to a file.
